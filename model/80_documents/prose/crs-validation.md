Validation asks whether a cook accepts the product, not whether the system requirements evaluate true. KT-VVP-001 describes that activity. No validation case is in the model yet, so this specification does not claim that a cook has accepted the timer.

Open points that affect the needs, and are not decided here:

- whether two years on the reference day is the life a cook actually expects;
- whether 70 dB at 0.3 m is loud enough in a real kitchen;
- whether a reflective display, with no backlight, is acceptable at the dark end of a counter.

Those points stay open until a cook reviews a representative unit. They are not resolved by the baseline analysis.
