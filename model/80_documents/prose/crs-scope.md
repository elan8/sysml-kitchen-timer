The product is a stand-alone kitchen timer. In scope is what the cook can set, read, hear and maintain: a countdown, a readable remaining time, an audible completion, and replacement of the cell without a tool. The product stays small, light and inexpensive enough for a countertop.

Out of scope are menus, a clock, a backlight, a rechargeable pack, a companion application, and any second appliance. The kitchen is the place the timer is used. It is not a system this specification constrains.

The system requirements that make these needs measurable are in KT-SRS-001. This document does not choose a display, a cell or an enclosure.
