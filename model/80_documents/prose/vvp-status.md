VER-001 is analysis. VER-002 is a test of the completion alarm. Its verdict is the record `run42 verify` writes to `verdicts.json` next to `Documents.sysml`, and this plan does not invent that record. At the baseline recorded in `project.md` the integration asserts evaluate true, including life, pulse current, timing, readability, loudness, envelope, mass, cost, cell replacement, and the three fit checks. That status is a property of the model. It is not a verification record with an operator, a date and a configuration hash.

Still open before any release claim:

- no laboratory measurement of current, loudness or contrast;
- no validation case and no cook's review;
- loudness remains an estimate, so the loudness assert can pass while a kitchen trial fails;
- the reference day is an assumption a cook has not accepted.

The requirements verification matrix is complete for the twelve system requirements: each is verified by VER-001. The completion alarm is also the objective of VER-002. Completeness of that matrix is not completeness of the evidence.
