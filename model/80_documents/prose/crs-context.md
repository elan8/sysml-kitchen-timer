The only stakeholder in the model is the cook: the person who sets the timer, reads it, hears it and replaces the cell. There is no installer, service technician or remote operator.

The cook and the timer exchange button commands and the remaining-time image. Replacing the cell is a use of the timer. The cell is not an external power supply, and nothing in the product charges it.

Ordinary kitchen light is the reading condition. A normal working distance in the same room is the hearing condition. The reference day used later for battery life is an engineering assumption, recorded in KT-SDS-001. It is not itself a customer requirement.
