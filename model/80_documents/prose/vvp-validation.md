Validation is a cook using a representative timer and accepting or rejecting each customer requirement. It is not implied by VER-001. The model has no validation case, so this plan has no validation matrix and claims no acceptance.

The activity that would close each need:

| Customer requirement | Acceptance activity |
| --- | --- |
| STK-001 Readable countdown | The cook reads the remaining time at 0.5 m in ordinary kitchen light, including a dim counter, without a backlight and without leaning over the timer. |
| STK-002 Long operating life | The cook is shown the reference day and the resulting life, and accepts that day as ordinary use. A heavier day the cook actually keeps is a change to the assumption, and the life is recalculated. |
| STK-003 Audible completion | The cook hears completion from a normal working distance in a kitchen, with the enclosure closed. |
| STK-004 Replaceable cell | The cook replaces the cell with no tool and uses the timer again. |
| STK-005 Simple countdown | The cook sets, starts, pauses, resumes and cancels without instruction, and accepts the size, mass and cost of the unit. |

Each activity needs a unit. Until that unit exists, these rows are the plan, not a result.
