The four documents of the kitchen timer form one set. Each holds one kind of information and refers to the others for the rest.

| Number | Document | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KT-CRS-001 | Customer Requirements Specification | What does the cook need? |
| KT-SRS-001 | System Requirements Specification | What shall the timer do, and how well? |
| KT-SDS-001 | System Design Specification | How is the timer built to do it? |
| KT-VVP-001 | Verification and Validation Plan | How will we know that it does? |

The structure of the set follows these guides, tailored to a product of this size:

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018, *Systems and software engineering — Life cycle processes — Requirements engineering*, for the stakeholder and system requirements specifications.
- NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev 2, *NASA Systems Engineering Handbook*, for the verification and validation plan and the requirements verification matrix.
- INCOSE, *Systems Engineering Handbook*, fifth edition, and INCOSE, *Guide to Writing Requirements*, for the separation of needs, requirements, design and verification.

The model is checked with Spec42 0.60.2. The documents are rendered with Doc42 from `model/80_documents`.
