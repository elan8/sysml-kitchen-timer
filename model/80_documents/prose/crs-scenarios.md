Seven scenarios cover a countdown and the replacement of the cell. The cook sets a duration, starts it, may pause and resume, may cancel, hears completion, dismisses the alarm, and can replace the cell without a tool. The table lists those scenarios as they are written in the model.

Setting, starting, pausing, resuming and cancelling are meant to stay obvious: one control to raise the setpoint, one to lower it, one to start, pause and resume, and one to cancel or dismiss. The scenarios do not assign those controls to particular buttons. That assignment is design.
