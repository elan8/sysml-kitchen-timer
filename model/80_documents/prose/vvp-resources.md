VER-001 needs the model, Spec42 0.59.2, and the domain libraries that release bundles. It needs no chamber, meter, fixture or prototype. The command and the evaluated headline results are recorded in `project.md`.

A later measurement campaign, not yet planned as cases, would need:

- a current meter that can resolve the idle current of a few microampere as well as the alarm current;
- a sound-level meter at 0.3 m, with the timer in its enclosure;
- a way to read the display at 0.5 m under about 100 lux without adding a backlight for the test;
- a scale, calipers, and one CR2032 of the capacity the model assumes.

No facility is booked. No instrument list is controlled. Those resources are named so the gap is visible.
