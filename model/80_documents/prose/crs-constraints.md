The following bounds are part of the customer need for a simple countertop timer, and KT-SRS-001 makes them numeric:

- the countdown runs from one second through 99 minutes 59 seconds;
- the product fits a small counter, stays under 100 g, and its illustrative component cost stays within a single-digit euro budget;
- the cell lasts through ordinary use for at least two years and can be replaced without a tool;
- remaining time is readable without leaning over the timer, and completion is audible from a normal working distance.

The reference usage behind "ordinary use", the illuminance behind "ordinary kitchen light", and the distance behind "a normal working distance" are assumptions of the design baseline. They are stated with the analyses in KT-SDS-001. Changing them changes the evidence, not the wording of these needs.

Illustrative part costs are planning figures. They are not supplier quotes, and the manufacturer names in the model are placeholders.
